Our Story

About Creating Smiles

Creating Smiles is a neighbourhood dental clinic in Krishna Nagar, built around one simple idea: a visit to the dentist should feel calm, clear and caring. We take the time to listen, explain and treat every patient as an individual.

The clinic is led by Dr. Divya Bhardwaj, who focuses on comfortable, personalised dental care for patients and families in and around Shani Bazar Gali.

Whether you are coming in for a routine check-up or have a concern you would like looked at, you are welcome to call us and book a consultation.
